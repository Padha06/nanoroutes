01 The problem

Neurological diagnosisstill runs on guesswork.

Millions of neuropathic and neurodegenerative cases are caught late, read subjectively and recorded inconsistently. The signal that predicts disease years earlier is buried in scans, waveforms and notes that no clinician can hold in mind at once.

Nanoroutes exists to close that gap — to route the noise of neurology into a clear, repeatable and explainable signal. Not a black box that guesses. A system that shows its reasoning.

02 The system

The NeuroInformation System

NIS is the core of Nanoroutes: one pipeline from capture to decision. It is built to be multimodal, explainable and deployable — from a research lab to a clinic.

NIS · Capture
01 / Capture

Capture

Imaging, electrophysiology and clinical variables gathered into a single, governed record — the raw material of every decision.

02 / Interpret

Interpret

Models trained across neurological cohorts surface early, subtle and non-obvious markers that a single reader would miss.

03 / Explain

Explain

Every output is traceable to the evidence behind it. No unexplained verdicts, no black boxes a clinician has to take on faith.

04 / Act

Act

Outputs are formatted for real clinical workflows, so insight converts into earlier, better intervention.
